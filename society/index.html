The Society

A serialized story about an artist, his anonymous identity, and the emergence of a digital resistance movement.

Originally released through Creative Bytes in July 2023, this 16-chapter work follows Charles and his pseudonymous identity, Jxsper. The story explores surveillance, authorship, anonymity, and the human consequences of technological power.
