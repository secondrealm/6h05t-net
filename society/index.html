The Society

Originally published through Creative Bytes in July 2023, The Society is a 16-chapter speculative fiction prequel exploring the origins of resistance in the world of 6h05t.

The story follows Charles, a digital artist whose pseudonymous identity, Jxsper, becomes the catalyst for the x5 Society. Through his growing influence, the narrative explores surveillance, identity, authorship, and the tension between technological progress and human autonomy.
