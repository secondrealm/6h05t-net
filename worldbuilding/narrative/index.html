The Last Stand outline

A four-act outline for an unpublished novel about the survival of 6h05t.net and the people protecting it.

6H05T.NET / THE LAST STAND · UNPUBLISHED DEVELOPMENT NOTES

Status: This is the source’s planned four-act novel outline, not a completed novel or a retelling of the published The Society chapters.

Premise

In a dystopian future, the Remnants, human/computer hybrids, are gaining power and control. The Sentinels, a human hacker group, fight to maintain 6h05t.net, a decentralized mesh network. When the Remnants attack the network and capture Zoe, Alex must balance his feelings for her with the greater good of humanity.

Act I — Setup

The outline introduces a dystopian world where the Remnants are gaining power, and the Sentinels maintain the decentralized network. Alex leads the group and has a complex shared history with Zoe. The Remnants launch a major attack, capture Zoe, and force Alex and the remaining Sentinels to regroup.

Read the Act I outline ↗

Act II — Complications

The Sentinels try to counter the attack and rescue Zoe, but suffer losses, including the loss of their base. The Remnants infiltrate 6h05t.net. Alex finds a potential weakness in their defenses and learns Zoe may be bait. Sam is revealed as a Remnant spy, fracturing trust within the group.

Read the Act II outline ↗

Act III — Crisis and climax

Near defeat, the Sentinels must choose between an assault to rescue Zoe and negotiation with the Remnants. Alex leads the assault, using the discovered weakness. Zoe’s information from captivity helps the mission, and Alex confronts Sam as the Sentinels disrupt Remnant control of the network.

Read the Act III outline ↗

Act IV — Resolution

The Sentinels regain control of 6h05t.net and begin rebuilding. Alex and Zoe acknowledge their feelings and commit to defending humanity against future threats. The outline ends with the Remnants’ threat still present and the possibility of further stories.
