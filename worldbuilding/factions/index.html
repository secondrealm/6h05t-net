Factions and characters

The original outline places a human hacker collective against a class of technologically augmented rulers.

6H05T.NET / THE LAST STAND · UNPUBLISHED DEVELOPMENT NOTES

The Sentinels

The Sentinels are a human hacker resistance group committed to protecting the decentralized mesh network, 6h05t.net. They oppose the Remnants’ consolidation of political and informational power, while navigating difficult questions about loyalty, sacrifice, and collective survival.

The Remnants

The Remnants are human–computer hybrids who seek dominant control over society and the network. Their advantages include advanced technology and proposed systems of neural manipulation. The working notes also suggest that some Remnants might question their faction's goals, but this remains a possible subplot rather than an established character arc.

Character concepts / The Last Stand

Alex

The proposed protagonist and leader of the Sentinels. He resists enforced integration with technology and must weigh his obligations to the group against his relationship with Zoe. The outline centers his moral dilemma between rescuing one person and protecting the network.

Zoe

A fellow Sentinel with a complex personal history with Alex. Her capture in the opening attack creates the central personal stakes. In the planned climax, information learned during captivity helps the resistance.

Sam

Alex’s trusted ally, later revealed in the outline as a Remnant spy. His betrayal destabilizes the Sentinels and creates a crisis of trust; possible redemption was explored in subsequent subplot notes, but not established as the story's outcome.

Separate narrative branch: Charles / Jxsper and the x5 Society belong to the published serial The Society. The development notes here do not establish a one-to-one correspondence between those characters and Alex, Zoe, or Sam.

Additional proposed characters

The working material raises possibilities including a remorseful engineer, a sympathetic Remnant, a hidden society, a new Sentinel recruit, and a double agent. These were prompts for exploration, not completed characters.
