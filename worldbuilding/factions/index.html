Factions and characters

The original outline places a human hacker collective against a class of technologically augmented rulers.

6H05T.NET / THE LAST STAND · UNPUBLISHED DEVELOPMENT NOTES

The Sentinels

In a dystopian future where human/computer hybrids known as the Remnants are vying for world domination, a group of human hackers called the Sentinels fight back to retain their humanity and freedom. Their battlefield is 6h05t.net, a decentralized mesh network that holds the key to global control.

The Remnants

In a dystopian future, the world is at the brink of total control by the Remnants, a faction of human/computer hybrids.

Core character roles in the outline

Alex

We meet Alex, our protagonist, during a tense operation. He's a skilled hacker, leader of the Sentinels, and a man with a deep-seated resentment against the Remnants. We see him interacting with his team, indicating a strong bond and mutual respect.

Zoe

We meet Zoe, a fellow Sentinel, during the same operation. She's a strong, intelligent woman with a shared history with Alex. Their chemistry is palpable, but there are hints of a complicated past.

Sam

Alex's closest ally, Sam, is revealed to be a Remnant spy, causing a major setback to their plans and creating a crisis of trust within the Sentinels.

Optional character directions

  • A subplot could follow an engineer or scientist who originally contributed to the technology that led to the Remnants' creation, now filled with regret and seeking redemption by secretly aiding the Sentinels.
  • A faction within the Remnants might start to question the path they're on, leading to internal conflicts and potential alliances with the Sentinels.
  • A character believed to be a loyal member of the Remnants could be revealed as a double agent working for the Sentinels, adding an element of espionage and intrigue.
  • A former Sentinel who left the group due to ideological differences could return, causing tension and conflict within the group, but ultimately contributing to their cause in a significant way.
  • The Sentinels could discover a hidden society of humans who have managed to avoid detection by the Remnants. This society could provide unexpected aid, or could have their own agenda, complicating the Sentinels' mission.
