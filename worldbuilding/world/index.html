The world

A cyberpunk society divided by access to technology, where the last open communications network becomes a site of resistance.

6H05T.NET / THE LAST STAND · UNPUBLISHED DEVELOPMENT NOTES

World premise

In the world of 6h05t.net: The Last Stand, the advent of the Remnants and the 6h05t.net has created a society that straddles the line between the physical and the digital, a world that is both familiar and alien.

Physical world

Cities have evolved into sprawling cyberpunk landscapes. The infrastructure blends old and new: crumbling buildings outfitted with high-tech devices, neon lights illuminating the streets, and towering digital billboards advertising cybernetic enhancements. The divide between wealthy and poor is stark, with the rich living in high-tech fortresses while the less fortunate reside in crowded, run-down sectors.

Technology

Artificial intelligence, robotics, and cybernetics play a significant role. The Remnants, human/computer hybrids, have access to cutting-edge technology that they use to exert control and maintain power. The notes propose neural control technology that allows them to manipulate and control others as a primary method of domination.

6h05t.net

The decentralized mesh network, maintained by the Sentinels, is an open platform for free communication and information. The notes describe it as the last beacon of the free internet and a symbol of resistance against Remnant control. The Sentinels use it for coordination; ordinary people use it for uncensored news and connection.

Culture and social structure

Society is highly stratified. The Remnants form the ruling class and live in luxurious enclaves, while ordinary people face a more difficult existence. The Sentinels are few but are seen as symbols of hope and resistance. Cybernetic enhancements are common among the privileged, but controversial, with many fearing the loss of their humanity.

History

The source notes describe the transition to a Remnant-dominated world as gradual. Human/computer integration was initially seen as a great leap forward. As the Remnants gained power and began controlling society, dissent grew. The Sentinels formed from that dissent and created 6h05t.net in response to Remnant control over information and communication.

Daily life

For most people, life is a struggle to maintain independence and humanity under Remnant control. Those who can access 6h05t.net use it to stay informed and connected. Many also fear being transformed into a Remnant against their will.

Politics

The political landscape is dominated by the Remnants, with the Sentinels at the heart of a growing resistance. The notes also mention smaller factions with their own agendas, adding complexity to the political situation.

Sensory direction

The source suggests sensory details such as neon-lit streets reflected on rain-soaked pavements, the hum of electric cables, ozone mixed with street food, cold steel against crowded marketplaces, synthetic food, and the digital hum of the city. These are writing prompts, not all established facts about the setting.
