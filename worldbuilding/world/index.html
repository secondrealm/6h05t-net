01 / setting and systems

The world

A cyberpunk society divided by access to technology, where the last open communications network becomes a site of resistance.

6H05T.NET / THE LAST STAND · UNPUBLISHED DEVELOPMENT NOTES

Physical environment

The working notes imagine sprawling cities where older infrastructure coexists with advanced machines. Neon advertising, high-tech fortresses, and deteriorating districts make the divide between wealthy and poor visible in the built environment.

The contrast is deliberate: privileged residents occupy highly technologized enclaves, while less affluent communities inhabit crowded sectors and face greater constraints on everyday life.

Technology

Artificial intelligence, robotics, cybernetic enhancements, and human–computer integration shape this setting. The Remnants, a faction of human–computer hybrids, possess advanced systems that help them consolidate power. An important proposed mechanism is neural control technology, allowing manipulation of other people.

The network: 6h05t.net

6h05t.net is a decentralized mesh network maintained by the Sentinels. The notes describe it as an uncensored channel for communication, information, and resistance coordination, and as a symbol of human autonomy in a world of increasingly centralized control.

Core premise: The central struggle is not simply over a website, but over who controls communication, knowledge, and the boundaries of human agency.

Social structure

Remnants occupy the ruling class in this proposed future. Cybernetic enhancements are common among the privileged, but controversial elsewhere. The Sentinels form a relatively small counterforce, while ordinary people navigate inequalities in resources, surveillance, and access to uncensored information.

History and politics

According to the working chronology, human–computer integration was initially celebrated as progress. Over time, the expanding power of the Remnants produced dissent. The Sentinels formed in response, maintaining 6h05t.net as a counterweight to control over information and communication.

The political system is described as Remnant-dominated, with smaller groups and movements pursuing their own interests. The notes leave those other factions open for further development.

Everyday life

Most people struggle to retain independence. Those able to access 6h05t.net use it to stay informed and connected. A fear of involuntary transformation into a Remnant shadows daily life in the original development material.
