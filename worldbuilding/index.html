Worldbuilding

A record of the fictional systems, characters, and narrative experiments developed for 6h05t.net: The Last Stand.

The worldbuilding archive documents the original development of 6h05t.net: The Last Stand, a dystopian science-fiction concept created in 2023 through iterative human–AI collaboration.

After developing this imagined future, the project expanded into an exploration of its origins. That creative direction led to The Society, a 16-chapter prequel published in July 2023.

These materials preserve the original setting, characters, factions, themes, and narrative possibilities. They represent the development of the larger fictional universe, rather than a definitive account of events established in the published chapters.
