Subplot ideas

The source document brainstorms possible additions to the four-act outline. These are exploratory suggestions, not established plot events or completed character arcs.

6H05T.NET / THE LAST STAND · UNPUBLISHED DEVELOPMENT NOTES

  1. Rebel within the Remnants. A Remnant begins to question the group’s mission and their own humanity, eventually choosing whether to help the Sentinels.
  2. Sentinel betrayal aftermath. The group struggles with guilt or suspicion after Sam’s betrayal and works to function as a team again.
  3. The origins of 6h05t.net. A backstory explores the creation and early days of the network and why the Sentinels defend it.
  4. Alex’s past. A past encounter with the Remnants or a personal loss could deepen Alex’s motivation.
  5. Zoe’s captivity. Zoe could learn about Remnant plans or weaknesses, form a dangerous alliance, or attempt an escape.
  6. Romantic tension. Alex and Zoe navigate doubt, jealousy, or misunderstanding amid the conflict.
  7. Remnants’ leader conflict. The Remnants’ leader struggles to maintain control over increasingly independent hybrids.
  8. The resistance movement. Ordinary citizens might begin resisting the Remnants, inspired by the Sentinels.
  9. The engineer. An engineer or scientist who contributed to the Remnants’ technology regrets their role and secretly aids the Sentinels.
  10. The spy. A Sentinel works undercover among the Remnants, with their loyalty tested as they grow sympathetic to the cause.
  11. Family ties. A Sentinel discovers a loved one has chosen to become a Remnant.
  12. A new recruit. A new Sentinel’s training and growth provide a fresh perspective on the group.
  13. The outside world. Other groups, safe havens, or communities living under threat could widen the view beyond the Sentinels.
  14. The traitor’s redemption. Sam regrets betraying the group and seeks redemption, possibly through a sacrifice.
  15. Remnants’ dissension. A Remnant faction questions its direction, creating internal conflict or possible alliances.
  16. Forgotten origins. Old technology or knowledge could create a race between the Sentinels and Remnants.
  17. Personal vendetta. A Sentinel’s loss could turn the conflict into a personal pursuit of revenge.
  18. The double agent. A character believed loyal to the Remnants is revealed to be working for the Sentinels.
  19. The prodigal Sentinel. A former Sentinel returns after leaving over ideological differences, bringing new tension and potential support.
  20. A hidden society. The Sentinels discover a human community that has avoided the Remnants, with its own agenda and possible aid.
