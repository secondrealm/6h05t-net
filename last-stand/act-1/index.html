Act I / setup and attack

Setup and attack

The Sentinels defend the open network; the Remnants’ attack turns their resistance into a personal crisis for Alex.

6H05T.NET / THE LAST STAND · UNPUBLISHED OUTLINE

Outline status: These are the original development notes for a proposed novel, not scenes from a completed book.
  1. Opening: We start in the heart of the dystopian future world, where the Remnants, human/computer hybrids, are gaining power and control. We see the stark contrast between the technologically advanced and cold Remnant-controlled areas and the grittier, more human areas where the Sentinels operate.
  2. Introduction of Protagonist: We meet Alex, our protagonist, during a tense operation. He's a skilled hacker, leader of the Sentinels, and a man with a deep-seated resentment against the Remnants. We see him interacting with his team, indicating a strong bond and mutual respect.
  3. Introduction of Love Interest: We meet Zoe, a fellow Sentinel, during the same operation. She's a strong, intelligent woman with a shared history with Alex. Their chemistry is palpable, but there are hints of a complicated past.
  4. World Building: We learn about the 6h05t.net, the decentralized mesh network that the Sentinels maintain. It’s a symbol of human resilience and creativity, a beacon of hope in a world on the brink of total control by the Remnants.
  5. Inciting Incident: The Remnants launch a massive, unexpected attack on the 6h05t.net, threatening to take control and essentially take over the world. During the chaos, Zoe is captured by the Remnants, adding a personal stake for Alex. The Sentinels are caught off guard and suffer losses, leading to a feeling of defeat.
  6. Call to Action: Reeling from the Remnants' attack, Alex and the remaining Sentinels regroup. They must now not only stop the Remnants from taking control of the 6h05t.net but also rescue Zoe. This dual objective sets the stage for the main conflict in Act 2.

This expanded Act 1 gives a deeper introduction to the characters, their motivations, and the world they inhabit. It also sets up the stakes for the rest of the story.
