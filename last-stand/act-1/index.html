Act I / setup and attack

Setup and attack

The Sentinels defend the open network; the Remnants’ attack turns their resistance into a personal crisis for Alex.

6H05T.NET / THE LAST STAND · UNPUBLISHED OUTLINE

Outline status: These are the original development notes for a proposed novel, not scenes from a completed book.
  1. Opening. Begin in a dystopian future where the Remnants, human/computer hybrids, are gaining power. Show the contrast between technologically advanced, cold Remnant-controlled areas and the grittier, more human places where the Sentinels operate.
  2. Introduction of the protagonist. Introduce Alex during a tense operation. He is a skilled hacker, leader of the Sentinels, and opposed to the Remnants. His interactions with the team establish their bond and mutual respect.
  3. Introduction of the love interest. Introduce Zoe, a strong and intelligent fellow Sentinel with a shared history with Alex. Their chemistry is apparent, alongside hints of a complicated past.
  4. The network. Establish 6h05t.net as the decentralized mesh network maintained by the Sentinels and a symbol of human resilience and creativity.
  5. Inciting incident. The Remnants launch an unexpected attack on 6h05t.net. Zoe is captured, the Sentinels suffer losses, and the group faces the threat of the Remnants taking control.
  6. Call to action. Alex and the remaining Sentinels regroup. Their goals are now to stop the takeover and rescue Zoe.
