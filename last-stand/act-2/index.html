Act II / complications

Complications

The Sentinels lose ground as the Remnants infiltrate the network and betrayal breaks trust within the group.

6H05T.NET / THE LAST STAND · UNPUBLISHED OUTLINE

Outline status: These are the original development notes for a proposed novel, not scenes from a completed book.
  1. New plan. Alex and the Sentinels devise a plan to counter the attack and rescue Zoe. The group recognizes the scale of the challenge but remains determined.
  2. First confrontation. The Sentinels launch a counterattack. They make some progress but are pushed back, suffer significant losses, and lose their base of operations.
  3. Midpoint reversal. The Remnants infiltrate 6h05t.net, causing worldwide havoc. Alex discovers a potential weak spot in the Remnants’ defenses and learns Zoe may be used as bait.
  4. Personal despair. Alex glimpses Zoe in captivity, looking defeated yet defiant. His helplessness and guilt intensify his determination.
  5. Betrayal. Sam, Alex’s closest ally, is revealed as a Remnant spy. The betrayal devastates the group and creates a crisis of trust.
  6. Lowest point. The Sentinels are fractured and feel defeated. Alex carries the weight of the group’s situation as the Remnants near their goal; the team retreats to regroup.
