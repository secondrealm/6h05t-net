Act IV / recovery and uncertain peace

Resolution

The Sentinels regain the network and begin rebuilding, while the outline leaves the Remnants’ threat open.

6H05T.NET / THE LAST STAND · UNPUBLISHED OUTLINE

Outline status: These are the original development notes for a proposed novel, not scenes from a completed book.
  1. Immediate aftermath. After disrupting Remnant control, Alex, Zoe, and the remaining Sentinels retreat from the base. Their victory is tempered by remembrance of fallen comrades.
  2. Resolution of the love story. Once safe, Alex and Zoe acknowledge their feelings and decide to face the uncertain future together.
  3. Beginning to rebuild. The world starts to recover from the Remnants’ infiltration of 6h05t.net. The Sentinels’ actions bring a renewed sense of unity among the remaining human population.
  4. New equilibrium. The Sentinels establish a new base to defend the network. Alex and Zoe take leadership roles within the group.
  5. Seeds of a sequel. The outline ends with the Remnants still a threat and the Sentinels committed to defending humanity against future attacks.
