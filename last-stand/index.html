The Last Stand

A dystopian science-fiction concept about human/computer hybrids, a resistance, and the fight to retain humanity and freedom.

6H05T.NET / THE LAST STAND · CONCEPT AND OUTLINE ONLY

Development status: This page preserves an original concept, synopsis, and four-act outline. It is not a completed or published novel.

Description

In a dystopian future where human/computer hybrids known as the Remnants are vying for world domination, a group of human hackers called the Sentinels fight back to retain their humanity and freedom. Their battlefield is 6h05t.net, a decentralized mesh network that holds the key to global control.

Alex leads the Sentinels through escalating conflicts against the Remnants. When Zoe, Alex’s complex love interest and a fellow Sentinel, is captured, the conflict takes on personal stakes. Betrayal within the Sentinel ranks and the threat of Remnant control further complicate their mission.

Synopsis

The Remnants, a faction of human/computer hybrids, are on the brink of controlling the world. The Sentinels, led by Alex, resist their advance and maintain 6h05t.net as a decentralized mesh network and beacon of hope.

When the Remnants attack the network and capture Zoe, Alex must balance his feelings for her with the greater good of humanity. A betrayal from within fractures the Sentinels. Alex and the group rally, use a discovered weakness in the Remnants’ defenses, and attempt to rescue Zoe and regain the network. The outline ends with the world beginning to rebuild and the Remnants’ threat still unresolved.

Four-act outline

  1. Act I — Setup and attack

    The Sentinels defend the network; the Remnants attack and capture Zoe.

  2. Act II — Complications

    The resistance suffers losses, the network is infiltrated, and Sam is revealed as a spy.

  3. Act III — Crisis and assault

    Alex chooses an assault to rescue Zoe and confront the Remnants and Sam.

  4. Act IV — Resolution

    The Sentinels regain the network, begin rebuilding, and prepare for continuing threats.
